An R&D professional who works across the whole food chain.

I work in food research and development, with experience in snack seasoning, bakery products and laboratory quality systems. That mix lets me think about a product as a flavor idea, a formula, a production run and a documented, controlled process.

Today I develop new seasonings for snack products at PT Siantar Top Tbk. Earlier I improved and developed bakery products at production scale, and began my career in quality assurance, where I learned how much good documentation matters.

My day-to-day sits between the lab and the plant: planning trials, testing materials, refining formulas and writing things down clearly so the next person can repeat the result.
